👀 What Guardian checks — and what it doesn't

  • ✅ Which apps are open and for how long (for time limits)
  • ✅ Which network you're on (for school / home rules)
  • ✅ The screen, only to spot danger: strangers asking for secrets or photos, bullying, scams, adult or gambling sites
  • ✅ Your parent gets a message only when something looks risky
  • 🚫 No constant recording — safe screens are deleted instantly
  • 🚫 Your parent doesn't read all your chats
  • 🚫 Nothing is sold or used for ads
  • 🗑️ Everything is deleted after 7 days
  • 🌳 The more trust you build, the more freedom you get
