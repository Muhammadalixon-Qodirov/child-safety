🔒 Privacy by design

  • No constant recording: screenshots are kept only when the AI flags risk (≥ 60).
  • Parents see blurred previews first and the AI's explanation, not full chat logs.
  • Adult or sexual content is never saved or forwarded; you get a text alert only.
  • Everything is deleted automatically after 7 days. No ads, no third-party trackers.
  • Your child can always see what Guardian checks in their own dashboard.
  • Children are never penalised for being targeted: only tampering lowers their trust score.
