Guardian

AI that steps in when a child is in danger online — and stays out of the way when they're not. Smart app & site limits, location-aware modes, and risk-only screenshots, with a dashboard for parents and kids.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent dashboard 🧒 Child dashboard

Tip: in the parent dashboard press ▶ Run demo scenario to watch the AI pipeline work on synthetic screens.

🧠

Two-stage AI

A cheap on-device check looks at every screen; only suspicious ones go to Groq vision + a policy-following safety model (gpt-oss-safeguard).

📍

Wi-Fi zones

School Wi-Fi turns on school rules; an unknown café network turns on strict mode and tells the parent.

🗓️

Schedules & limits

School time, homework, bedtime and per-app daily limits. Apps close automatically; kids can ask for more.

📲

Telegram in one tap

Alerts arrive with a blurred screenshot and buttons: keep blocked, it's OK, or "how do I talk about this?".

🌱

Trust that grows

Kids earn trust points and bonus time. Being targeted is never punished; only tampering is.

🔒

Privacy by design

No constant recording, blurred previews, no storage of adult content, 7-day auto-delete, and full transparency to the child.
